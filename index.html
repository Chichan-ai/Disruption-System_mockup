How approval works

  • Your request is queued under Records → Approval for an admin to review.
  • Once approved, the branch's map pin, status metrics, and the active alert feed update automatically — no page reload needed.
  • Rejected requests stay visible in Archive with the reviewer's note.
  • For life-safety emergencies, also call the regional hotline directly.
